## 🩺 Healthcare Projects  

### 🫁 PulmoScan-CNN  
A **deep learning system** for **cardiopulmonary diagnostics** using Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs).  
- Implemented a model capable of analyzing medical images to detect pulmonary conditions.  
- Built with **Python, TensorFlow, and Keras**, and optimized for healthcare datasets.  
- Goal: assist radiologists in **early detection of lung and heart diseases**.  

---

### 🧠 ASTRAI – AI Framework for Medical Data  
A project focused on **building AI solutions for healthcare applications**.  
- Designed to process and analyze medical imaging (DICOM).  
- Combines **data engineering pipelines** with **deep learning** for segmentation and anomaly detection.  
- Includes a module for **oncology diagnostics** through image segmentation.  
- Built in collaboration with healthcare professionals.  

---

### 📊 Healthcare Data Architecture & NAS  
- Developed a **NAS + Cloud hybrid architecture** to store and manage large-scale medical datasets.  
- Integrated **MySQL + cloud servers** for structured access to clinical data.  
- Ensured **secure, remote, and scalable access** for AI models and healthcare research.  
- Designed with **MLOps principles** for real-time deployment of medical AI models.  

---

### 📐 Mathematical & Physics Models for Healthcare  
- Simulated complex systems such as **double pendulums** to model chaotic behavior.
